It follows Israel's history from beginning to end: the kingdoms of Israel and Judah, the Romans and the diaspora, Herzl and Zionism, the Balfour Declaration and the British Mandate, the Holocaust, the 1947 UN partition plan, 1948 (Israel's independence and the Palestinian Nakba), the Six-Day War, the Yom Kippur War, peace with Egypt, the intifadas, the Oslo Accords, Gaza and the war that began on October 7th, 2023.

It tells both sides' stories. Death tolls are given with their source, because many of them are still disputed.
