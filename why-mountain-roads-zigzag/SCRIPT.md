# Why Mountain Roads Zigzag

Script for the owner to record. About 220 words, about 63–66 seconds at a natural pace (the spike video is 66 s).

## Hook options (pick one for line 1)

1. **"The richest man in the mountains built a road straight down, and it delivered his whole fortune in one morning."** (recommended: the payoff line calls back to it)
2. "He built the fastest road in the Alps, and it was the most expensive mistake he ever made."
3. "The slowest road in the mountains took three hours, and it's the only reason one man kept his cargo."

## Script (one line per sentence, read top to bottom)

1. The richest man in the mountains built a road straight down, and it delivered his whole fortune in one morning.
2. Every cart in the village took the old road.
3. Forty bends, three hours to the bottom.
4. Rudolf, the richest merchant, called it a waste of a mountain.
5. So he blasted his own road. Straight down. One hour, top to bottom.
6. And at the bottom, he built his warehouse, full of every barrel he owned.
7. Old Hans just kept driving the bends, sipping his coffee.
8. The whole village called him the snail of the mountain.
9. First month, Rudolf's carts arrived twice as fast.
10. Second month, they burned through their brakes.
11. Then came the first ice of winter.
12. Rudolf loaded his biggest cart and sent it straight down.
13. Hans turned into the first bend.
14. In real life, the steeper the road, the harder the hill pulls you down.
15. A zigzag is the same mountain, cut into gentle slices.
16. Like stairs and a slide. On stairs, you can stop. On a slide, you can't.
17. Halfway down, Rudolf's brakes started to smoke.
18. The wheels locked, and the cart slid like a sled.
19. It didn't stop. It went straight through the warehouse doors.
20. Three hours later, Hans rolled in. Not a drop spilled.
21. And Rudolf's road had delivered his whole fortune, in one morning.

## How it follows the reference

| Reference beat | This script |
|---|---|
| Hook: a strange act | A road built straight down |
| The village mocks the hero | "The snail of the mountain" |
| **Plant the danger early** | Line 6: the warehouse full of barrels at the bottom |
| Fool vs. calm hero | Rudolf's straight road vs. Hans and his coffee |
| Rule of three | First month, second month, then the ice |
| In-world explainer + 2 comparisons | Gentle slices; stairs vs. slide |
| Payoff shown with spectacle | Smoking brakes, sliding cart, crash through the doors |
| Ironic callback | "delivered his whole fortune, in one morning" |

The physics is real: a zigzag spreads the same height over a longer, gentler road, so the pull
down the slope is smaller and the brakes and horses can hold the cart. The village and people are
invented.

## Recording tips

- Record the whole script in **one take**, as a continuous story, like the reference narrator.
- **Calm and dry**, slightly amused. Don't shout; let the words carry the drama.
- Small pause after line 1, after "Then came the first ice of winter.", and before the last line.
- Speed up a little on lines 17–19 (the crash), then slow down for the last two lines.
- Quiet room, phone close to your mouth, no music. Send it as **MP3 or WAV**.
- If you change any words while recording, that's fine; I'll match the captions to what you say.
