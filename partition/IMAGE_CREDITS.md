# Image sources and licences (Bharat Ka Batwara 1947)

Rule for this video: only **public domain** images (no CC-BY / CC-BY-SA, no news-agency or stock photos,
no clips from other YouTube videos or films). All files come from Wikimedia Commons, where each one
carries a public-domain tag. Indian photographs from 1947 are public domain in India (photographs enter the
public domain 60 years after publication); paintings and maps from the 1800s and 1909 are public domain worldwide.
Gory images (bodies, blood) are deliberately not used, so the video stays inside YouTube's
violent / graphic content guidelines; violence is told through narration, ruins, refugees and maps.
