ABOUT THIS VIDEO
Around 1280, on a dusty hillside near Söğüt, a chieftain named Osman had no city and no palace. His descendants would take Constantinople, reach the gates of Vienna twice, and rule from Algeria to Iraq and from Hungary to Mecca.

We follow the rise: Osman and Orhan, Bursa, the devşirme system that turned Christian boys into Janissaries and grand viziers, Bayezid the Thunderbolt and his defeat by Timur, the civil war between brothers, Mehmed II and the fall of Constantinople in 1453, Selim the Grim's conquest of Egypt and the holy cities, and Süleyman the Magnificent at Mohács, Vienna and the height of Ottoman power.

Then the long decline: the cage, Lepanto, the failed siege of Vienna in 1683, the Tulip Era and the coffeehouses, wars with Russia, the Tanzimat reforms, the "sick man of Europe", Abdülhamid II, the Young Turks, Gallipoli and the First World War, and finally Mustafa Kemal and the Republic of Turkey.
