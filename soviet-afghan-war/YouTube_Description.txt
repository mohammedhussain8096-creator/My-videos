ABOUT THIS VIDEO
In 1979 the Soviet Union sent 80,000 men into Afghanistan, expecting to stay a few months. They stayed nine years. This video follows the whole war: the 1978 communist coup in Kabul, the murder of Taraki, the KGB plot against Hafizullah Amin, the Mujahideen's war in the mountains, Ahmad Shah Massoud in the Panjshir Valley, Operation Cyclone and Pakistan's ISI, the arrival of Osama bin Laden, the Stinger missiles of 1986, Gorbachev's decision to leave, and the last Soviet general crossing the Friendship Bridge on 15 February 1989.

Then what came after: the fall of the Soviet Union, the Mujahideen civil war, the rise of the Taliban, the killing of Massoud two days before 9/11, and twenty years of a new superpower in Afghanistan.
