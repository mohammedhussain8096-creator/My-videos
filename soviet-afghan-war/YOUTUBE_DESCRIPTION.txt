DESCRIPTION
On February 15th, 1989, a Soviet general walked alone across the Friendship Bridge, and not a single Soviet soldier was left behind him in Afghanistan. Nine years earlier, 80,000 troops had rolled in expecting a few months of guard duty. Instead they walked into a trap that helped bring down their entire empire.

This is the full history of the Soviet-Afghan War. It covers the KGB plot to poison Hafizullah Amin, Ahmad Shah Massoud ("the Lion of Panjshir"), the CIA's Operation Cyclone, the Stinger missiles that took the sky from the Soviets, and the "Cargo 200" coffins sent home in secret. It ends with the collapse of the USSR, the rise of the Taliban, and the road to 9/11 and America's own twenty-year war.
