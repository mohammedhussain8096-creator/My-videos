ABOUT THIS VIDEO
In 1812 the most powerful man in Europe marched into Russia and took Moscow, then lost almost his entire army. In 1941 Hitler studied that mistake and walked into the same trap. This video explains why.

We follow Napoleon from the Neman River to burning Moscow and the Berezina, then look at what really protects Russia: the largest country on Earth, stretching across 11 time zones; seven centuries of survival, from the Mongols and the Time of Troubles to Stalingrad and the collapse of the Soviet Union; the largest proven natural gas reserves on the planet; the longest Arctic coastline and the world's only nuclear-powered icebreakers; the largest nuclear arsenal; and the nation that sent the first satellite and the first human into space.

And because power has many faces, we also look at the other side: a smaller economy than the United States or China, a shrinking population and heavy sanctions.
