# The Clever Gap: channel description

Paste the text between the lines into YouTube Studio → Customization → Basic info → Description.

---

Every odd little detail was somebody's clever fix.

The Clever Gap makes short animated stories about the strange designs hiding in plain sight: why train tracks have gaps, why coins have ridges, why some light bulbs screw in backwards. Each one starts with a person, a problem, and a fix that looks completely wrong, until it isn't.

Every story ends on the real reason the trick works. In under a minute you'll find out how steel grows in the summer sun, or how one tiny change stopped a whole city of thieves, without the story ever stopping to lecture you.

The characters are made up. The tricks are real.

New Shorts every week. Watch one, then tell us which everyday thing you want explained next.
